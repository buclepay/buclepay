# Security

buclePay never holds funds: payments go from the payer's wallet to the merchant in one transaction. Still, we take every report seriously.

- Report vulnerabilities privately to **security@buclepay.com** (English or Spanish), or through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability". Please do not open public issues for security problems.
- We reply within 72 hours and keep you informed until it is fixed.
- Scope: the SDKs in `sdk/` and the examples. Out of scope: social engineering, third-party wallets, token issuers (Tether, Circle).
- Please give us reasonable time to fix before disclosure. We credit researchers who want it.
